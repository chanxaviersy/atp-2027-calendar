# 📈 Live ATP Rankings

This site does not host ranking data directly (it would go stale within hours). Instead, it links to the authoritative sources that update in real time.

## 🌍 Official Sources

### Singles Rankings

- 🏆 **[ATP Official — Live Singles Rankings](https://www.atptour.com/en/rankings/singles)**
  *The authoritative source. Updated every Monday after tournament completion.*

- 📊 **[ATP Race to Turin (Live)](https://www.atptour.com/en/rankings/race-to-turin)**
  *Year-to-date points for the Nitto ATP Finals qualification race.*

### Doubles & Other

- 🤝 **[ATP Doubles Rankings](https://www.atptour.com/en/rankings/doubles)**
- 🎾 **[ATP Live Race (in-match points)](https://www.atptour.com/en/scores/live-flashscores)**
- 👥 **[ATP Doubles Race to Turin](https://www.atptour.com/en/rankings/doubles-race-to-turin)**

## 📅 How Rankings Work

- Players earn points at tournaments based on **category** and **result**.
- Points are kept on a **rolling 52-week basis** (oldest fall off each week).
- Best **18 tournament results** count for most players.
- The top **8 players** in the **Race to Turin** qualify for the **Nitto ATP Finals**.

## 🔄 Update Cadence

ATP rankings are updated **every Monday at ~12:00 UTC** after the previous week's tournaments conclude. The Race to Turin is updated **continuously** during tournament weeks.

## 🛠️ Developer Note

If you want to programmatically fetch ranking data, the ATP does not provide a public API. Most third-party apps use:
- Web scraping (fragile — terms of service may prohibit)
- Manually-maintained data (community projects like [Ultimate Tennis Statistics](https://www.ultimatetennistatistics.com/))

This site deliberately avoids scraping to remain a reliable, low-maintenance reference.
